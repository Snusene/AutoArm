# Auto Arm

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Snusene/AutoArm/develop/About/preview.png)

A RimWorld mod that makes your colonists pick their own weapons.

Plays nice with Combat Extended, Simple Sidearms, and most weapon mods. Supports RimWorld 1.5 and 1.6.

## Requirements

[Harmony](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077) needs to be loaded first.
